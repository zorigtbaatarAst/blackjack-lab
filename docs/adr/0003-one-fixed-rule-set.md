# One fixed rule set, no surrender or insurance

The Book depends on the table rules, and the Coach, the Drills and every recorded Decision are keyed to Chart cells of one specific Book. We therefore fix the rules to 6 decks, dealer stands on soft 17, Blackjack pays 3:2, double after split, re-split to 4 Hands, split aces get one card each with no re-split, dealer Peeks, and the Shoe reshuffles at a 75% Cut card. Players can't change them.

Surrender and insurance are left out on purpose. The product promise is the four Actions Hit, Stand, Double and Split, and every extra Action adds a new column to the Strategy chart, the Drills and the stats.

## Consequences

Adding configurable rules later means one Book per rule set, and accuracy stats split by rule set. Stats recorded before that change belong to this rule set.
